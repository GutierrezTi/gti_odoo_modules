Spanish IRPF Reconciliation Audit

Audit IRPF retentions, import AEAT data, and resolve discrepancies

This module allows Spanish companies to perform a comprehensive audit of their IRPF retentions by comparing Odoo accounting records with official data provided by the Tax Agency (AEAT).

  • Automatic calculation of recorded IRPF retentions per quarter or fiscal year.
  • Wizard to import AEAT Excel data (Model 190) and automatically match amounts.
  • Generate automated PDF reports with detailed invoice breakdowns for each client.

Client Portal & Communication

Streamline the resolution of mismatches with an integrated workflow:

  1. Notify Clients: Send automated emails with PDF breakdowns and a secure token link.
  2. Client Portal: Clients can click "I Agree" or "Report Error" directly from their browser without needing an Odoo user.
  3. Automatic Tracking: Client responses and reasons for discrepancy are instantly logged in the audit line chatter for your accounting team to review.
